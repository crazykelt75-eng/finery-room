Care Guide

Make your set last

Lashes

  • Keep them dry and away from steam on the first day.
  • Brush them gently each morning with a clean lash brush.
  • Use an oil-free makeup remover around your eyes.
  • Don't pull or pick at them. Book a removal (P30) instead.
  • Book your refill within 2 weeks (P40) to keep them full.

Nails

  • Wear gloves for washing up and cleaning.
  • Don't use your nails as tools to open tins or scratch off labels.
  • Rub a little cuticle oil in each day.
  • If a nail chips or breaks, don't pick at it. Book a repair (P10 per nail).

Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

How long does a lash set last?
It depends on your natural lashes and how you look after them. To keep your set looking full, book a refill within 2 weeks (P40).
When should I book a refill?
Within 2 weeks of your set. A refill in that time is P40, compared with P70 for a new Classic Set.
Do I need to pay a deposit?
A deposit may be required to secure your booking. If it is, we'll tell you when you book and send the payment details on WhatsApp.
What if I'm running late or can't come?
Please arrive on time, and message us on WhatsApp as soon as you know something has changed. No-shows will not be rebooked.
Can you remove my lash extensions?
Yes. Lash removal is P30.
How should I prepare?
For lashes, come with clean eyes: no mascara or eyeliner. For nails, tell us on WhatsApp what you'd like when you book, so we can plan your time.
