QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a classic lash set typically last?
A full classic set can last anywhere from 4 to 6 weeks, depending on your personal hair growth cycle and aftercare. However, because natural eyelashes naturally shed every single day, we recommend booking a 2-week refill (P40) to keep your lash line looking dense, full, and fluttery.
How should I prepare for my lash or nail appointment?
For lashes: Please arrive with clean eyes — free of mascara, liquid eyeliner, heavy oil creams, or strip lash adhesive residue. For nails: Arrive with bare nails, or notify us when booking if you need prior gel or acrylic removal so we reserve adequate time for a clean, safe soak-off.
What does the deposit cover and how do I pay?
The deposit confirms your booking slot in our calendar and is deducted directly from your service cost on appointment day. Once we confirm your desired date and time via WhatsApp (+267 765 841 00), deposit details (Orange Money, eWallet, or instant mobile transfer) will be shared.
How do I reschedule if something comes up?
We understand plans change! Simply send us a message on WhatsApp at least 24 hours in advance, and your deposit will roll over smoothly to your rescheduled date. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice or missed appointments cannot be refunded or rebooked.
Where in Tati Siding is Finery Room located?
We are located in New Stands, Tati Siding, Botswana. Once your appointment deposit is received, we share a precise WhatsApp pin location and driving landmarks for hassle-free arrival.
