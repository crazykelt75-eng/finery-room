# WhatsApp message templates

These are for the owner to send by hand. **Nothing here sends automatically.**

Save each one as a **Quick Reply** in WhatsApp Business: Settings → Business tools → Quick replies. Use the shortcut shown, then fill in the [brackets] before sending.

> Two blanks still need the owner:
> - **Deposit number:** not confirmed yet. The number supplied has 7 digits after +267, and Botswana mobile numbers have 8. Leave it out until it's checked.
> - **Deposit amount:** set per booking.

---

## 1. Booking confirmation — `/book`
Send when the time is agreed.

> Hi [Name], thank you for booking with Finery Room! 💜
>
> **[Service]** on **[Day, date]** at **[time]**
> 📍 New Stands, Tati Siding
>
> [Only if a deposit is needed:] To secure your slot, please pay a deposit of P[amount] by FNB PayToCell to [number] and send the payment screenshot here.
>
> Please arrive on time. If anything changes, just message us here. No-shows will not be rebooked.
>
> See you soon!

## 2. Day-before reminder — `/remind`

> Hi [Name], a quick reminder of your **[Service]** tomorrow, **[day]**, at **[time]** at Finery Room, New Stands, Tati Siding. 💅
>
> [For lashes:] Please come with clean eyes: no mascara or eyeliner.
>
> Reply **YES** to confirm, or let us know if you need to change your time.

## 3. Refill nudge, day 12 after a lash set — `/refill`

> Hi [Name]! It's been 12 days since your lash set. 💜
>
> Book your refill before the 2 weeks are up and it's just **P40**. A new Classic Set is P70.
>
> Would **[day]** at **[time]** work for you?

## 4. Thank-you after the visit — `/thanks`

> Thank you for visiting Finery Room today, [Name]! 💜 We hope you love your [nails / lashes].
>
> Here's how to make them last: https://finery-room.crazykelt75.workers.dev/assets/care-guide.pdf
>
> If you have a moment, we'd love to hear how your visit went. Just reply here.

*(A review link can be added here later, once one is set up.)*

## 5. Photo permission — `/photo`
Send before posting any client photo. Keep the reply as the written record of consent.

> Hi [Name], your [nails / lashes] came out beautifully! 💜 May we share a photo of them on our website and social pages? We'll only show [your hands / a close-up of your lashes], not your full face, unless you say that's fine.
>
> Reply **YES** if that's OK. No problem at all if you'd rather not.

---

### Weekly routine for refills
1. Each morning, look at who had a **lash set 12 days ago** in your booking chat or notebook.
2. Send them `/refill`.
3. Tag their chat with a **"Refill due"** label (Chats → Labels) so it's easy to see who hasn't booked yet.
