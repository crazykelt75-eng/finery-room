Lash & Nail
Aftercare Guide

A few simple habits keep your set looking fresh for longer. Keep this guide on your phone, and message us on WhatsApp any time you have a question.

Lashes

  • Keep them dry and away from steam on the first day.
  • Brush them gently each morning with a clean lash brush.
  • Use an oil-free makeup remover around your eyes.
  • Don't pull or pick at them. Book a removal instead.
  • Book your refill within 2 weeks to keep them full.

Nails

  • Wear gloves for washing up and cleaning.
  • Don't use your nails as tools to open tins or scratch off labels.
  • Rub a little cuticle oil in each day.
  • If a nail chips or breaks, don't pick at it. Book a repair.
